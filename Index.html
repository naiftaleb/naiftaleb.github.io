About Me

I am a Hematology and Immunohematology Specialist, Assistant Professor, and Global Health Researcher whose work bridges laboratory medicine, epidemiology, explainable AI, and evidence synthesis.

My research focuses on developing scalable, low-cost diagnostic strategies and translating evidence into practical health system solutions for fragile and conflict-affected settings.

Research Interests: Hematology · Immunohematology · Laboratory Medicine · Diagnostic Biomarkers · AI in Medicine · Explainable AI · Systematic Reviews · Meta-analysis · Global Health · Conflict Medicine · Women's Health · Infectious Diseases · Sickle Cell Disease · Health Systems Research
